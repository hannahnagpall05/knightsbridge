Spacious 4-bed residences and duplexes of 6,000 sq. ft. and 10,000 sq. ft. saleable area respectively. With just one apartment per floor, the project ensures complete privacy and exclusivity along with 360° views of the skyline.

The towers are designed by Hafeez Contractor, and the landscapes are crafted by Integral Designs.

Landscape amenities: sloping greens, pavilion, jogging track, yoga/meditation lawn, swimming pool, kids' pool, sit-out plaza, chaise loungers, outdoor gym, amphitheatre, celebration lawn, elderly park, dog's park, badminton court, tennis court, basketball court, cricket practice net and kids play area. The 50,000 sq. ft. clubhouse consists of 6 curated lifestyle zones: leisure, wellness, play, business centre, food and utility.
